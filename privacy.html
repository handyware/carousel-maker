Privacy Policy

Seamless Carousel Maker. Effective July 31, 2026.

Short version: Carousel Maker does not collect, store, or share your personal data or your photos. The rest of this page explains exactly what the app does and does not do.

Your photos

When you import a photo into Carousel Maker, it is processed entirely on your device. Carousel Maker never uploads your photos to a server, and we do not have access to them. Exported carousel slides are saved directly to your device's photo library. Nothing you create with the app leaves your device unless you choose to share or post it yourself.

Accounts

Carousel Maker does not require or offer an account, sign in, or login of any kind. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifier through the app.

Crash and performance diagnostics

Carousel Maker may use a third party service called Sentry to help us detect and fix crashes and technical problems. If enabled, this can include a crash report or performance trace, along with basic technical details such as your device model, operating system version, and app version. This diagnostic data does not include your photos or any content you create in the app. We do not use this data for advertising, and it is not linked to your identity.

Third party links

The app includes a link that opens Instagram in your browser or the Instagram app so you can post your carousel. Carousel Maker does not share any data with Instagram when you tap this link. Instagram's own privacy policy applies once you leave Carousel Maker.

Children's privacy

Carousel Maker is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and change the effective date above.
