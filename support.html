Support Seamless Carousel Maker Carousel Maker turns one wide photo into a seamless Instagram carousel. It slices your photo into slides so the cuts between them disappear when you swipe. Getting started Tap New, pick a shape (4:5 or 1:1), and add one wide photo. Tap Preview to check the seams before you export. Export, then post the slides to Instagram as a carousel, newest slide first.
